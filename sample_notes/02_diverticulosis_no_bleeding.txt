Patient is a 68-year-old male who underwent screening colonoscopy, which
revealed scattered diverticula throughout the sigmoid and descending colon.
No inflammation was seen, and there is no active or recent bleeding by exam
or history. Patient is entirely asymptomatic.

Assessment: Diverticulosis of the large intestine, without perforation or
abscess, without bleeding.

Plan: High-fiber diet counseling. Routine follow-up colonoscopy in 5 years.
