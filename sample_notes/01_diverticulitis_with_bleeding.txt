Patient presents with 2 days of left lower quadrant abdominal pain, low-grade
fever, and bright red blood per rectum noted this morning. CT abdomen/pelvis
shows inflamed diverticula in the sigmoid colon without evidence of
perforation or abscess.

Assessment: Acute diverticulitis of the large intestine, without perforation
or abscess, with associated lower GI bleeding.

Plan: Admit for IV antibiotics, serial hemoglobin checks, and GI consult.
